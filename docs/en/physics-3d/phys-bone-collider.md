# PhysBoneCollider

Deflectors for PhysBone chains: keep hair out of heads, capes out of legs.

| Inspector group / field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Shape: Shape Type | Sphere, capsule, plane |
| Radius / Height / Center | Shape dimensions |
| Direction / Plane Normal | Orientation |
| Behavior: Inside Bounds | How deep penetration resolves |
| Gizmos: Show Gizmo | Volume visualization |

Attach to the body part the chain must avoid; the chain slides around the volume at runtime.

Related: PhysBone, Armature, Sphere/Capsule Collider.
