# Spot Light

Cone light: flashlight, stage lamp, headlight. Direction from the Transform, cone from `spot_angle` / `spot_inner_angle`.

- Intensity in lumens, default 1000 (same scale as Point Light).
- Up to 4 simultaneous spot shadow maps (1024 default).
- Gizmo shows the cone with inner-angle falloff ring.

Aim with the Transform rotation; animate `spot_angle` for zoom effects and `intensity` for flicker.

Related: Light (Base), Shadows, Projector (textured cone).
