# Procedural Sky

Dynamic day-cycle sky: sun position, night stars, atmosphere tint — all computed, no textures.

| Inspector group | Covers |
|---|---|
| Procedural Night Sky | Night Sky toggle, Night Exposure |
| Stars | Density, Intensity, Scale, Twinkle, Twinkle Speed, Seed, Tint |
| Milky Way | Milky Way band controls |

A directional light with `procedural_sky_lighting` drives the sun: rotating the light moves the sun disc and rebalances ambient automatically.

Related: Skybox, Atmosphere, Clouds, Directional Light.
