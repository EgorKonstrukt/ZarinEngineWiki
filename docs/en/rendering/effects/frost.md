# Frost

Per-object effect through the ObjectFx pass (unlike PostFX, which processes the whole frame). Built on the shared ObjectEffect base; scripts drive it by animating progress/intensity floats.

## How it works

Icy rim and crystal growth creeping from edges with a progress control.

## Parameters

Progress, crystal density, tint.

## Tips

Freeze spells, cryo chambers, winter curse spreading. Reverses cleanly 1→0.

## Cost

Cheap.
